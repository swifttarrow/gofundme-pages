# Charity Partnership Program — Requirements

## Purpose

Charity onboarding is a separate program from the fundraiser starter flow.

## Why Separate

- Registered charity setup has legal and compliance requirements that do not apply to standard fundraiser creation.
- Charity onboarding requires partnerships with legal firms and related verification workflows.

## Program Requirements (Initial)

1. Define legal partner handoff flow for charity applicants
2. Capture required compliance documents and status checkpoints
3. Track verification outcomes before any "charity" label is granted
4. Keep charity onboarding outside the `/fundraiser/new` user path

## Out of Scope for Fundraiser Starter

- Nonprofit incorporation support
- Legal entity verification workflows
- Charity legal advisory steps
